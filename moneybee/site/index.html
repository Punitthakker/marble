Built for UAE payments

The patterns we watch for.

Our scenarios start from the risks we see in UAE acquiring, payouts and cross-border corridors, with hospitality and retail merchants at the core.

Structuring

Repeated payments sized to stay just under reporting or review thresholds.

Card testing

Bursts of tiny card-not-present attempts across many cards or one device.

Refund abuse

Refunds that exceed sales, go to new cards or follow chargeback spikes.

Transaction laundering

Volumes or ticket sizes that don't match a merchant's declared business.

Mule and pass-through

Funds in and straight out, with payouts to fresh or linked beneficiaries.

High-risk corridors

Exposure to sanctioned regions, high-risk jurisdictions or unusual routing.

How it works

From payment to decision in one call.

Monitoring sits inside the MoneyBee payment flow. Each event is scored against live scenarios and the decision comes back before the payment completes.

IngestPayment, payout or onboarding event arrives via API.
EnrichCustomer, merchant and history pulled from the data model.
DecideScenarios score the event: approve, review or block.
InvestigateReviews open as cases in the right inbox.
EvidenceOutcome, rationale and audit trail stored for reporting.
